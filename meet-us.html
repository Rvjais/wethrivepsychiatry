Mental Health Providers at Thrive Psychiatry

  • Jennifer Anderson, PMHNP-BC

    Mesa, AZ Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner

    Thrive Psychiatry


    My path to becoming a Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner was not the usual one. I was a junior high and high school teacher for 22 years. Eight of those years were spent teaching adolescents with emotional and behavioral disabilities. For five years, I was a reading specialist. The remaining years, I taught general education in all the core subject areas. I do truly love working with teens.

    Though I loved teaching, there came a time for change. I went to the University of Arizona for my Master’s in Nursing. As a registered nurse, I worked in local hospitals as a medical-surgical nurse, a travel nurse, and finally a drug and alcohol detox nurse.

    Aside from my professional experience in teaching and nursing, I also have a lot of family experience with ADHD, anxiety, and mood disorders. I have been on the inside and understand the impact mental health issues have on both the individual and the family.

    Becoming a Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner involved pulling together all of this experience in a way that I believe will help people the most. I don’t believe in just prescribing medication. Medication helps when appropriate, but therapy, learning strategies, and changing negative self-talk are just as important. I will partner with your therapist. Psychiatry blends together teaching, problem-solving, and listening in a way that I am able to help people become their best selves. I love helping others to thrive.

    When not working, I can be found enjoying the water or listening to live music with my friends and family. I love spending time with my adult children and adorable grandbabies.

  • Weston Hilland, PMHNP-BC

    Mesa, AZ Board-Certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

    Thrive Psychiatry


    Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner | U.S. Marine Corps Veteran

    Welcome, I’m glad you’re here. As a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner, I understand how life’s challenges, stress, and past experiences can affect our emotional health. My goal is to provide compassionate, evidence-based care that helps you feel understood, supported, and empowered in your journey toward balance and well-being.

    My time in the Marine Corps gave me a deep respect for the strength and resilience people carry, but also for the unseen struggles that can come with high expectations, trauma, or constant pressure. That experience continues to shape my approach to mental health care today: grounded in empathy, collaboration, and respect for each person’s unique story.

    I work with individuals of all ages and specialize in ADHD evaluations and treatment, as well as care for anxiety, depression, trauma, bipolar disorder, insomnia, and behavioral challenges. Whether you’re seeking help for yourself or your child, my goal is to create a safe, nonjudgmental space where you can talk openly and take meaningful steps toward feeling better.

    I also have a special interest in working with veterans, first responders, and individuals living with ADHD, who often face unique stressors and carry invisible burdens. Together, we’ll focus not just on managing symptoms, but on building tools and strategies to support long-term emotional health and resilience.

    Collaboration is central to how I practice. I work closely with primary care providers, therapists, and families to ensure that care is comprehensive and connected. I believe that when patients feel truly heard and supported by a team, they are better equipped to heal and thrive.

    Ultimately, I strive to make every patient feel comfortable, respected, and understood, because mental health care should always be a partnership built on trust, compassion, and hope.

  • Kacie Neilson, NCMA

    Mesa, AZ Nationally Certified Medical Assistant

    Thrive Psychiatry

    I have over 14 years of experience in patient care and clinical support across a variety of medical settings. My background includes hands-on assistance with providers in clinical and procedural environments, as well as experience supporting patient care in both medical and behavioral health settings.

    My experience includes working directly with device-based treatments and assisting with in-office procedures and patient preparation. I also support day-to-day clinical operations to help maintain smooth and coordinated patient flow.

    I value patient education and clear communication, with a focus on helping patients feel informed and comfortable throughout their care experience.
