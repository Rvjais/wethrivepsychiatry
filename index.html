Welcome to Thrive Psychiatry - Your Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner in Mesa, AZ

Today you are one step closer to a new you where you feel empowered and on a positive path to growth and well-being.

To thrive, we need stability in multiple areas. Picture a four-legged chair. One of those legs is our physical health. When we are active and healthy, we feel better. We have the energy to meet our daily responsibilities. We have the strength and endurance to engage and enjoy our family and friends. The second leg is our social health. Humans are social beings. Some of us need more, and some need less, but we all need connections to others. We need people to share with, to laugh with, and to cry with sometimes. Our functional health represents the third leg. Functional health includes being able to successfully work, paying your bills on time, and even keeping your car registration up to date.

That final leg is our mental health. People struggle with mental health for various reasons. Sometimes it is a short-term issue caused by an event, such as the loss of a loved one or a move to a new city. Other times, mental health issues are the result of chemical imbalances, trauma, or adverse circumstances, creating long-term management needs. Whatever the cause, stable mental health is needed to keep that chair from crashing down.

At Thrive, you will be listened to. A thorough evaluation will be done to understand your needs. You will be involved in creating your plan. I believe not only in medication when needed, but in learning strategies to manage difficulties, and changing negative behavior patterns and thoughts. Along with your therapist, I will help you get all four legs of the chair firmly on the ground. The goal is to thrive in all parts of your life!

We offer a full range of psychiatry services and treatments to improve your mental health, including ADHD, Anxiety, Depression, Bipolar Disorder, Behavioral Disorders, Schizophrenia, Insomnia, Mood Disorders, Anger Management, TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulator), Treatment-Resistant Depression, ExoMind, Cognitive Decline, EmTone, Wellness Treatment, Cellulite Reduction, and so much more. Most appointments at Thrive will be done through telemedicine. On Tuesdays and every other Saturday, in-person appointments are available. ADHD and Substance Abuse evaluations must be done in person. Follow-up appointments can be done through telemedicine.

Arizona In-person appointments - Every Tuesday 10 am - 8 pm and Every Other Saturday 10 am to 5 pm.
